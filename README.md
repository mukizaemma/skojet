## Skojet Boutique Retreat

Private luxury Boutique Hotel & retreat near Kinigi, Musanze — facing Rwanda’s volcanoes.

**Brand:** Private Luxury Beneath Rwanda's Volcanoes

### Stack

Laravel + Livewire public site, content-management admin, Tailwind/Vite for Jetstream pieces, Bootstrap/theme assets under `public/assets`.

### Brand layer

- `public/assets/css/skojet-theme.css` — forest green `#1F3A2E`, gold `#C8A977`, ivory `#F7F4EE`
- Typography: Playfair Display + Inter
- Copy: `lang/en/site.php` (French falls back where untranslated)

### Public IA

Home · Suites · Experiences · Dining · Gallery · Reviews · Location · Contact · Book Now

Suite URLs also accept `/suites` and `/suites/{slug}` (redirect to `/our-rooms`).
